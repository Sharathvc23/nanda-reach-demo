A company reaches a person's agent again, after the person moved.

Travel26 kept one thing about John: his NANDA locator. Not his card, not his inbox, not his endpoint. When it wants to reach him, it resolves the locator in the NANDA Index at send time and follows the routing rules on his card. John changes agents, declines a topic or moves his card, and the next message still lands where he said.

01 / How a send works

Four hops on every send. The company never stores the destination.

Only the locator persists on the company's side. Everything after it is looked up at send time, so the person's latest choices win.

1 · Locator

What Travel26 holds

A NANDA locator, given once over Poppy: in the client metadata document, or by calling the share_nanda_locator tool.

urn:air:host39.org:personal:<id>
2 · Index

Locator → card URL

One GET to the NANDA Index. The entry says where the person's agent card lives today and whether the entry is active.

GET /api/v1/resolve?locator=… → entry.registry_url, status
3 · Card

Agents and routing rules

An A2A agent card the person maintains. It lists their agents and which inbox each message topic goes to. none means: do not send.

routing: [ { match: "travel", deliver_to: "wajo" }, { match: "promotion", deliver_to: "none" } ]
4 · Inbox

The message lands

Travel26 writes to the inbox the first matching rule names, over InkBox. The trace of every hop is shown on its desk.

Route: [travel] → rule "travel" → John on Wajo InkBox: sent
02 / The five beats

What John changed, and what Travel26 saw next.

One change on John's side, then one send from Travel26 to the same stored locator. The dark column is Travel26's own log, abridged.

Beat 1 · Baseline

John's agent shops, and opts in

John's agent on Muse opens a signed-out Poppy session with Travel26, searches its MCP API and shares the locator. Travel26's first promotion goes to Muse under card v1. Muse

Rule in force: * → muse

Beat 2 · Second agent

John adds Wajo and reroutes travel

John tells his agent to add a second agent on Wajo and send travel there. His card goes to v2 with a travel rule. Travel26 sends again, to the same locator, and the message lands in Wajo. Muse is not contacted. Wajo

Rule in force: travel → wajo
Travel26 logIndex: match=exact status=active card=…/.well-known/agent-card.json Card: version 2, 2 agent(s), 2 rule(s) Route: topics [travel, promotion] -> rule "travel" -> John on Wajo InkBox: sent
Beat 3 · Declined topic

John says no to promotions

A promotion → none rule goes on the card. Travel26 reads it at send time and holds: nothing is sent, and the desk shows why. Travel messages are unaffected. held

Rule in force: promotion → none
Travel26 logCard: version 3, 2 agent(s), 3 rule(s) Route: topics [promotion] -> rule "promotion" -> none Held: John declines promotion. Nothing sent.
Beat 4 · Card move

John moves his card to a new URL

The card moves, John re-points his own Index entry, and the old URL answers 410. A merchant that saved the URL at first contact now has no path to John. Travel26 resolves the locator, finds the new URL and delivers. Wajo

Beat 5 · A third-party agent

Meta's Muse shares a locator by tool call

Not our agent: the production Muse agent shops at Travel26, is told about the share_nanda_locator tool, and calls it. Travel26 stores the locator and nothing else. No extension header was needed; the tool is the fallback for agents that do not declare it.

Human-prompted; the tool call is the agent's

03 / What the company keeps

Three fields. The rest is fetched at send time and not retained.

The extension says it in two rules: a Company MUST NOT store the card, the inbox address or the endpoint, and MUST honour none.

FieldKept by Travel26Why
client_idYesWhich client application the session belonged to; part of Poppy's session record.
subYesThe pseudonymous user id in the Poppy session, so repeat visits match up.
nanda_locatorYesThe one contact identifier. Resolvable by anyone, revocable and re-pointable by John alone.
Card URLNoLooked up in the Index on every send. Storing it is exactly what loses John in beat 4.
Agents, routing rulesNoRead from the card at send time; the desk shows them in the trace but they are not retained.
Inbox address, endpointNoKnown only for the duration of one send.
04 / Status

What this is, and what it is not.

What this is

  • A running reference implementation of the nandaindex.org/reach extension, version 1: a Poppy company (Travel26) and a person's agent (John's, on this host) with a card registered in the live NANDA Index.
  • Real mailboxes on InkBox. Every send on Travel26's desk shows its trace, including a control arm that fetches the URL a careless merchant would have saved.
  • Replayable. Beats 1 to 4 can be driven from John's console with the admin token; the card and the Index entry are the live ones.

What it is not

  • Not authorization, consent or confidentiality. The locator is resolvable by anyone who has it, and Poppy authenticates the client's assertion of the locator, not control of it. The claim is portable reachability.
  • Not freshness proof. Resolving on every send follows the person's latest choices; it does not prove the Index answer is current or authentic.
  • Known gap: the sender logs the Index entry's status but does not yet refuse entries that are not active, which the extension requires.
